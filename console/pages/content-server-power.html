Power

Graceful · the OS is asked; nothing happens if its AMT agent does not answer, and there is no silent fallback to a harder action

Other

Force · the OS is not asked; unsaved work is lost · each needs a hold to confirm

amt_power {device, action, confirmed: true} · one WS-MAN Digest attempt · default restart is graceful; hard_reset only when named

Advanced boot options (16) · the three marked in red act at that boot and take the hold to confirm
lock_power_button lock_reset_button lock_sleep_button lock_keyboard user_password_bypass forced_progress_events configuration_data_reset bios_pause reflash_bios use_safe_mode use_sol use_ider secure_erase enforce_secure_boot

Every option starts from its safe default on each call; only what you tick here applies. The three in red make the request itself destructive: the sheet becomes a hold to confirm when any of them is ticked, whatever button opened it.

amt_set_next_boot {device, target, options, confirmed: true} then amt_power · the combined entries (Reset to BIOS, Power up to PXE…) are these two calls in order, and the second is sent only if the first was accepted. The chip reads the BIOS-setup and IDE-R flags of AMT_BootSettingData; a PXE or disk request lives in the boot order, which that read does not show.
